Giveth House style guide

Built on the Giveth Style Guide 2.0 so we feel like family, with our own logo and our frames. The written rules are in STYLE_GUIDE.md.

01 Logo

Two colors, two versions

One SVG that takes the color of its container. Full logo for big uses, compact for header and footer.

Black on white (21:1)
Black on Dandelion (15:1)
White on Grape (7.35:1)
White on Indigo (16:1)
White on black (21:1)
Compact, for header and footer (min 96 px)

Clear space

An empty margin on every side equal to the height of the capital G in "Giveth" (about 17% of the logo width).

Never

Stretch, skew, rotate, box, outline, add effects, rearrange the frames, recolor it (it is black or white, nothing else), or put it on busy photos.
